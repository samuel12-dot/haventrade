## Welcome to HavenTrade

HavenTrade is a neighbourhood marketplace built for Abuja residents. It lets you buy and sell pre-loved, handmade, and second-hand goods with people who live close to you — safely, simply, and without the hassle of dealing with strangers far away.

Whether you are clearing out your home, looking for a bargain, or running a small side business, HavenTrade gives you everything you need in one place.

## 1. Getting Started

### Creating an Account

You need a HavenTrade account to buy or sell. Creating one is free and takes less than a minute.

**Steps:**

1. Open HavenTrade in your browser
2. Click **Sign Up** on the landing page
3. Fill in the registration form:
   - **Full Name** — your first and last name (displayed to other users)
   - **Email Address** — used to log in; must be unique
   - **Password** — at least 8 characters; choose something you will remember
   - **Postcode** *(optional)* — helps show listings near you
   - **Neighbourhood** *(optional)* — select your Abuja neighbourhood from the dropdown
   - **I want to sell on HavenTrade** *(optional)* — tick this box if you plan to sell items. You can always enable this later from your profile
4. Click **Create Account**

You will be taken directly to the home feed once your account is created.

> **Tip:** Even if you are not sure about selling yet, you can leave the seller checkbox unticked. You can upgrade to a seller account at any time from your Profile settings.

---

### Signing In

If you already have an account:

1. Click **Sign In** on the landing page or navigation bar
2. Enter your **Email Address** and **Password**
3. Click **Sign In**

You will be taken to the home feed. Your session is saved automatically — you will stay signed in even if you close the browser tab, until you choose to sign out.

> **Forgotten your password?** See the Profile & Settings section for how to change your password.

---

### Signing Out

1. Click your **account icon** (top right of the navigation bar)
2. Select **Sign Out**

You will be returned to the landing page. Your cart and wishlist are saved locally, so they will still be there if you sign back in on the same device.

---

### Navigating the App

The navigation bar appears at the top of every page and contains:

| Icon / Link | What it does |
|---|---|
| **HavenTrade logo** | Returns you to the home feed |
| **Search bar** | Search for listings by keyword |
| **Heart icon** | Opens your wishlist (saved items) |
| **Cart icon** | Opens your shopping cart; the number badge shows how many items are in it |
| **Person icon** | Access your profile, dashboard (sellers), and sign out |

On smaller screens, the navigation condenses into a compact layout. All icons remain accessible.

## 2. Browsing & Discovering Listings

### The Home Feed

The home feed is your main browsing page. It shows all available listings across Abuja, sorted by newest first by default.

Each listing card shows:
- A **photo** of the item
- The **title**
- The **price** (in Naira)
- A **condition badge** (New, Like new, Good, Fair, For parts)
- The **seller's avatar** and name
- A **heart icon** to save the item to your wishlist

Click any card to open the full listing detail page.

---

### Filtering Listings

You can narrow down the home feed using the filter bar at the top of the page.

**Available filters:**

| Filter | Options |
|---|---|
| **Category** | Furniture, Electronics, Vintage, Kids & Baby, Clothing, Books & Media, Home & Garden, Free |
| **Condition** | New, Like new, Good, Fair, For parts |
| **Price range** | Set a minimum and/or maximum price |
| **Neighbourhood** | Filter to listings in a specific Abuja neighbourhood |
| **Sort by** | Newest, Oldest, Price: Low to High, Price: High to Low |

**How to apply a filter:**

1. Click the filter or category chip you want
2. The page updates immediately — no need to press a separate search button
3. To remove a filter, click the same chip again or clear the filter field

You can combine multiple filters. For example, you can browse **Furniture** in the **Wuse II** neighbourhood priced between **₦10,000** and **₦50,000**.

---

### Searching

To search for something specific:

1. Click the **search bar** in the navigation bar
2. Type your search term (e.g. "vintage lamp", "baby crib", "MacBook")
3. Press **Enter** or click the search icon

The search results page will show listings that match your query. If nothing comes up, try a shorter or different keyword.

> **Tip:** Search works across listing titles and descriptions. If you are looking for something specific, try the item's material or style (e.g. "teak", "rattan", "leather") rather than just the product name.

---

### Viewing a Listing

Click on any listing card to open its detail page. Here you will find:

- **Photos** — swipe or click through all the seller's images
- **Title and price**
- **Condition badge** — a colour-coded indicator of the item's condition
- **Description** — the seller's full details about the item
- **Delivery information** — whether the seller offers cargo bike delivery, self-delivery, or pickup
- **Estimated delivery** — the seller's stated delivery time (e.g. "today", "1–2 days")
- **Item details** — dimensions, weight, materials (for physical items); format and licence (for digital items)
- **Seller card** — the seller's name, neighbourhood, rating, and a link to their full storefront
- **Related listings** — other items from the same seller or category

From this page you can:
- **Add the item to your cart** using the Add to Cart button
- **Adjust the quantity** using the + and − buttons before adding
- **Save the item** to your wishlist using the heart icon

## 3. Your Wishlist

The wishlist lets you save items you are interested in so you can come back to them later.

**To save an item:**

- Click the **heart icon** on any listing card (home feed, search results) or on the listing detail page

The heart turns filled/active to confirm the item is saved.

**To view your wishlist:**

- Click the **heart icon** in the navigation bar

Your wishlist shows all your saved items as listing cards. You can click any card to go back to its detail page, or click the heart again to remove it from your wishlist.

**To remove an item from your wishlist:**

- Click the **heart icon** on the listing card or detail page again

> **Note:** Your wishlist is linked to your account and will be available on any device you sign in on. It is also stored locally on your current device so it loads instantly even before the page finishes loading.

## 4. Shopping & Checkout

### Adding Items to Your Cart

1. Open a listing's detail page
2. Use the **+ / −** buttons to set the quantity you want (default is 1)
3. Click **Add to Cart**

A confirmation notification will appear briefly at the top of the screen. The cart icon in the navigation bar will update to show the new item count.

> **Note:** You do not need to be signed in to add items to your cart. However, you will need to be signed in to complete checkout.

---

### Reviewing Your Cart

Click the **cart icon** in the navigation bar to open your cart.

Your cart shows:
- Each item with its photo, title, and price
- A quantity adjuster (+ and −) for each item
- A **Remove** button to delete an item from your cart
- A **subtotal** at the bottom

**To change quantity in the cart:**
- Click **+** to increase or **−** to decrease the quantity
- The subtotal updates automatically

**To remove an item:**
- Click the remove/delete button next to the item

When you are happy with your cart, click **Proceed to Checkout**.

---

### Completing Checkout

Checkout is split into four steps. You can go back to a previous step at any time by clicking the back arrow.

---

**Step 1 — Review Your Order**

A summary of everything in your cart. Check item names, quantities, and prices before continuing.

Click **Continue** when ready.

---

**Step 2 — Delivery Address**

Enter where you want your items delivered:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Full Name** | The name of the person receiving the delivery |
| **Street Address** | House number and street name |
| **Postcode** | Your area postcode |
| **City** | Defaults to Abuja |
| **Delivery Notes** *(optional)* | Any instructions for the delivery rider (e.g. "Call on arrival", "Green gate") |

All required fields must be filled in before you can continue. If you miss a field, it will be highlighted in red.

Click **Continue** when done.

---

**Step 3 — Choose Delivery Slots**

If your order contains physical items, you will see a delivery slot picker for each seller in your order. This lets you choose a delivery time window that suits you.

- Slots are organised by seller — if you are ordering from two different sellers, you will see two slot pickers
- Click your preferred time slot to select it
- Digital items (PDFs, files) do not require a delivery slot

Click **Continue** when you have selected your slots.

---

**Step 4 — Payment**

Review the final order breakdown:

| Line | Description |
|---|---|
| **Subtotal** | Total cost of all items |
| **Delivery fee** | ₦1,500 per seller with physical items (free for digital items) |
| **Platform fee** | HavenTrade service fee |
| **Total** | The final amount you will pay |

Select your payment method:
- **iDEAL**
- **Card**

Click **Place Order** to confirm.

> **Note:** Payment processing is currently in demo mode. Placing an order will mark it as paid immediately for demonstration purposes.

---

### Order Confirmation

After placing your order you will be taken to the **Order Confirmation** page, which shows:

- Your unique **order number** (format: `HT-2026-XXXX`) — keep this for reference
- A summary of everything you ordered
- Your delivery address
- The total amount charged

Your cart is automatically cleared after a successful order.

## 5. Tracking Your Orders

To see all your past and current orders:

1. Click your **account icon** (top right)
2. Select **My Orders**

The order history page lists all your orders, with the most recent at the top.

**Each order card shows:**
- Order number
- Date placed
- Items ordered (with photos)
- Total paid
- Order status

**Order statuses explained:**

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Pending** | Order placed; waiting for the seller to begin packing |
| **Active** | Seller has started processing your order |
| **Out for delivery** | Your item is on its way to you |
| **Delivered** | Your item has been delivered |
| **Cancelled** | The order was cancelled |

Click any order card to view its full details, including the delivery address and per-item status.

> **Tip:** Once your order is delivered, you can leave a review for the seller directly from the order detail page.

## 6. Seller Profiles & Storefronts

Every seller on HavenTrade has a public storefront page where you can learn more about them before buying.

**To visit a seller's storefront:**
- Click the seller's **name or avatar** on any listing card or listing detail page
- Or browse to **Sellers** from the navigation and click any seller card

**A storefront contains four tabs:**

| Tab | What you will find |
|---|---|
| **Listings** | All the seller's active items for sale |
| **About** | The seller's tagline, neighbourhood, languages, opening hours, and workshop address |
| **Reviews** | Ratings and written reviews left by past buyers |
| **Policies** | The seller's delivery, returns, and condition policies |

The seller's overall **star rating** and **total number of reviews** are displayed prominently at the top of their storefront.

**Browsing all sellers:**

1. Click **Sellers** in the navigation bar (or on the landing page)
2. Browse the list of all sellers, sorted by rating
3. Use the **neighbourhood filter** to find sellers near you
4. Click any seller card to visit their storefront

## 7. Becoming a Seller

You can start selling on HavenTrade in two ways:

**Option A — At registration:**
- Tick the **"I want to sell on HavenTrade"** checkbox when creating your account

**Option B — From your profile (any time):**

1. Click your **account icon** and select **Profile**
2. Scroll down to the **Seller Settings** section
3. Toggle on **"Become a Seller"**
4. Click **Save Changes**

Once you are a seller, you will have access to:
- The **Seller Dashboard**
- The **Create Listing** editor
- **Seller order management**

> **Tip:** Complete your seller profile before listing your first item. Add a tagline, your neighbourhood, and your opening hours. Buyers are more likely to purchase from sellers with complete profiles.

---

### Completing Your Seller Profile

A strong seller profile builds trust with buyers. To complete it:

1. Go to **Profile** from your account icon
2. Fill in the **Seller Profile** section:

| Field | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **Tagline** | A short sentence about what you sell (e.g. "Handmade ceramics from Abuja") |
| **Neighbourhood** | Helps buyers find you by location |
| **Since (year)** | Shows how long you have been selling |
| **Workshop / Collection Address** | Where buyers can pick up items if you offer pickup |
| **Opening Hours** | Lets buyers know when you are available |
| **Languages** | Helps buyers who prefer to communicate in Igbo, Yoruba, Hausa, etc. |

3. Click **Save Changes**

## 8. Managing Your Listings

### Creating a Listing

1. Go to your **Seller Dashboard** (account icon → Dashboard)
2. Click **+ New Listing**
3. Fill in the listing form:

---

**Section 1 — Basic Details**

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Title** | A clear, descriptive title (e.g. "Vintage Teak Armchair — Excellent Condition") |
| **Price** | In Naira (₦). Enter `0` for free items |
| **Category** | Choose the most relevant category from the dropdown |
| **Condition** | Select the condition that best describes your item |

**Condition guide:**

| Condition | When to use it |
|---|---|
| **New** | Unused, with original packaging |
| **Like new** | Used once or twice, no visible wear |
| **Good** | Normal wear and tear, fully functional |
| **Fair** | Visible signs of use but still works well |
| **For parts** | Not fully functional; sold for components or repair |

---

**Section 2 — Description**

Write a detailed description of your item. A good description answers:
- What is it exactly?
- What is its history (how long have you had it, where did you get it)?
- Are there any flaws, scratches, or missing parts?
- What is included in the sale?

> **Tip:** Honest, detailed descriptions get more sales and better reviews. Buyers appreciate knowing exactly what they are getting.

---

**Section 3 — Photos**

Upload up to **6 photos** of your item. Clear photos are the single biggest factor in getting a sale.

**To upload a photo:**
1. Click the **Upload Photo** button
2. Select an image from your device (JPEG, PNG, or WebP; maximum 5 MB per photo)
3. Repeat for additional photos

**Photo tips:**
- Take photos in good natural light
- Show the item from multiple angles (front, back, sides, any flaws)
- Include a photo of any tags, labels, or original packaging
- Avoid cluttered backgrounds — a plain wall or floor works well

---

**Section 4 — Item Details** *(optional but recommended)*

| Field | When to use |
|---|---|
| **Dimensions** | For furniture and large items (e.g. "120cm × 80cm × 75cm") |
| **Weight** | For items where weight affects delivery |
| **Materials** | For furniture, clothing, or handmade items (e.g. "Solid teak wood") |

For **digital items** (PDFs, templates, files):

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Format** | File type (e.g. PDF, JPEG, ZIP) |
| **Licence** | Usage rights (e.g. "Personal use only") |

---

**Section 5 — Delivery Options**

Tick all the delivery options you offer:

| Option | Description |
|---|---|
| **Cargo bike delivery** | You or a rider will deliver to the buyer (recommended) |
| **Self delivery** | You handle delivery yourself |
| **Buyer pickup** | Buyer comes to collect from you |

Also set an **estimated delivery time** (e.g. "today", "1–2 days", "3–5 days").

---

**Section 6 — Neighbourhood & Location**

Select the **neighbourhood** where the item is located. This helps buyers filter listings near them.

---

**Publishing your listing:**

- Click **Publish** to make the listing live immediately
- Click **Save as Draft** to save it without publishing (you can publish it later)

---

### Editing a Listing

1. Go to your **Seller Dashboard**
2. Find the listing you want to edit
3. Click the **Edit** button (pencil icon) on the listing card
4. Make your changes in the editor
5. Click **Save Changes**

You can edit any field at any time — price, description, photos, delivery options, and status.

**Changing a listing's status:**

| Status | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Active** | Listing is visible to buyers in the feed |
| **Draft** | Listing is hidden from buyers; only you can see it |
| **Sold** | Listing is marked as sold and removed from the active feed |
| **Archived** | Listing is hidden and stored for your records |

---

### Removing a Listing

1. Go to your **Seller Dashboard**
2. Find the listing
3. Click the **Delete** button (bin icon)
4. Confirm deletion in the prompt

> **Warning:** Deletion is permanent. If you just want to temporarily hide a listing, change its status to **Draft** or **Archived** instead.

## 10. Managing Orders as a Seller

When a buyer places an order containing your item, you will see it appear in your **Dashboard** and your **Seller Orders** list.

**To view your orders:**

1. Go to your **Dashboard**
2. Click **View All Orders** or navigate to **My Orders → Seller Orders**

---

### Processing an Order

Each order shows:
- The buyer's name and delivery address
- The items they ordered (from you)
- Their selected delivery slot
- The current status of each item

**To update an item's status:**

1. Open the order
2. Find the item you are updating
3. Click the **Update Status** button
4. Select the new status from the dropdown

**Item status steps:**

| Status | When to set it |
|---|---|
| **Pending** | Default when the order is placed — you have not started yet |
| **Packed** | You have packed the item and it is ready for dispatch |
| **Out for delivery** | The item is with the rider or on its way to the buyer |
| **Delivered** | The item has been received by the buyer |
| **Cancelled** | You are unable to fulfil this item |

> **Note:** Once all items in an order reach **Delivered** status, the overall order is automatically marked as Delivered. This also enables the buyer to leave you a review.

---

### Best Practices for Sellers

- **Update order statuses promptly** — buyers appreciate knowing where their items are
- **Pack items carefully** — especially fragile or valuable items
- **Communicate via your storefront info** — keep your opening hours and contact details up to date so buyers can reach you
- **Honour your delivery slots** — buyers selected a time that suits them; respect it

## 11. Leaving a Review

After your order has been delivered, you can leave a review for the seller. Reviews help other buyers make informed decisions and reward good sellers.

**To leave a review:**

1. Go to **My Orders** and open the delivered order
2. Click **Leave a Review**
3. Select a star rating from **1 to 5**:

| Stars | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ | Excellent — exceeded expectations |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐ | Good — happy with the experience |
| ⭐⭐⭐ | Average — met basic expectations |
| ⭐⭐ | Below average — some issues |
| ⭐ | Poor — significant problems |

4. Write a short description of your experience *(optional but helpful)*
5. Click **Submit Review**

Your review will appear on the seller's storefront immediately and their overall rating will update automatically.

> **Note:** You can only leave one review per order. Reviews cannot be edited after submission, so take a moment to make sure you are happy with what you have written.

## 13. Frequently Asked Questions

---

**Q: Is HavenTrade free to use?**  
A: Creating an account and browsing is completely free. A small platform fee is added at checkout to cover the cost of running the marketplace.

---

**Q: How do I know if a seller is trustworthy?**  
A: Check the seller's storefront for:
- Their star rating and number of reviews
- Written reviews from past buyers
- How long they have been on the platform ("Since" year)
- A complete profile with neighbourhood, opening hours, and a tagline

Sellers with many positive reviews and a complete profile are generally a good sign.

---

**Q: Can I buy from multiple sellers in one order?**  
A: Yes. You can add items from different sellers to the same cart and check out in one go. You will see separate delivery slot pickers for each seller, and a delivery fee is calculated per seller for physical items.

---

**Q: What happens if a seller does not fulfil my order?**  
A: If a seller is unresponsive or fails to deliver, you can use the contact information on their storefront to reach them. Order dispute resolution features are planned for a future update.

---

**Q: Can I sell digital products?**  
A: Yes. When creating a listing, toggle on the **Digital item** option. Digital listings do not require delivery slots or a delivery fee. The buyer receives the file or access details directly.

---

**Q: What categories can I sell in?**  
A: Furniture, Electronics, Vintage, Kids & Baby, Clothing, Books & Media, Home & Garden, and Free. Choose the category that best describes your item.

---

**Q: How many photos can I add to a listing?**  
A: Up to 6 photos per listing. Each photo must be under 5 MB in size.

---

**Q: Can I edit my listing after publishing it?**  
A: Yes. You can edit any field of a listing at any time from the Seller Dashboard. Changes take effect immediately.

---

**Q: Can I delete my listing?**  
A: Yes. From your Dashboard, click the delete (bin) icon on the listing. This action is permanent. If you want to temporarily hide a listing, set its status to **Draft** instead.

---

**Q: How do I know when a buyer has ordered from me?**  
A: New orders appear in your Seller Dashboard immediately. Check your dashboard regularly. Order notifications by email are planned for a future update.

---

**Q: What does "For parts" condition mean?**  
A: It means the item is not fully functional and is being sold for its components or for someone who can repair it. Always be honest about an item's condition — it protects you and the buyer.

---

**Q: Can I save my cart and come back later?**  
A: Yes. Your cart is saved automatically on your device. It will still be there when you return, even if you close the browser or sign out and sign back in.

---

**Q: My images are not loading — what should I do?**  
A: Check your internet connection. Images on HavenTrade are served from a global image delivery network and should load quickly on any connection. If images consistently fail to load, try refreshing the page.

---

**Q: I forgot my password — what do I do?**  
A: Currently, you can change your password from your **Profile** page if you are already signed in. A password reset by email feature is planned for a future update. If you are locked out, contact support.

---
